Prepare Better, Feel Better! 🌸

📅 Manage Your Time

Make a realistic timetable and divide large topics into smaller tasks.

📖 Revise Regularly

Revise important concepts and practice questions regularly.

😴 Get Enough Sleep

Maintain a healthy sleep routine during exam week.

☕ Take Short Breaks

Take short breaks between study sessions to refresh your mind.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water and eat regular meals.

💗 Stay Positive

Focus on your progress instead of comparing yourself with others.
